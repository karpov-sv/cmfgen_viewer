Line and display settings

Uploaded samples and fitting intervals stay fixed. Models and stellar markers are redshifted in vacuum, then converted to the upload's Air/Vacuum convention. Wavelengths is locked to the active upload property; edit it on the upload page. Without uploads, Air is the default for model comparisons. Line windows span ±150 Å about the displayed, redshifted line position.

Telluric bands shade approximate O₂ and H₂O absorption regions in the selected Air/Vacuum convention. They stay fixed as source velocity changes. Guides assume topocentric uploaded wavelengths; prior barycentric/heliocentric corrections are not inferred. They do not correct absorption or mask fitting samples.

One wavelength and optional label per line; spaces or commas separate them. Input defaults to Air; saved lists retain their selected convention. Blank lines and # comments are ignored. Enable Spectral lines to show markers, then choose All lines or Custom lines. Reused across viewers in this browser.

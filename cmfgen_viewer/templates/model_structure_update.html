Scope and initialization
Guided updates support mass-loss, core-radius, VINF/BETA, and depth-count changes. Grid operations can also resample, add points, or recalculate sigma directly from the current structure. Any required RMAX adjustment or NP increase to cover ND + NC is included in the review. Applying archives binary checkpoints and radiation/matrix caches. It does not export populations or prove hydrostatic equilibrium. Review initialization compatibility before running.

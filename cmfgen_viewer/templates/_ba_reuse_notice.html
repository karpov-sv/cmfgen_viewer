Fresh BA on every full iteration. With COMP_BA=T and N_FIX_BA=0, CMFGEN rebuilds the BA/Jacobian matrix each time. This helps recovery and verification but can slow main computations. Stable continuations can try N_FIX_BA=1 with FIX_BA=0. Auto mode can test limited reuse after sustained healthy progress, with rollback if the trial fails. Automatic mode restores the starting reuse settings after successful verification.
